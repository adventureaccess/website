Why Bhutan, and why on foot

The Himalaya without the expedition

Your Asia-Pacific map today is Japan, New Zealand and Thailand. Bhutan would be the first Himalayan country on it, and it is the easiest one to start with: the trails are on foot, the valleys are green, and the days are long walks between monasteries, farms and villages rather than high-altitude pushes.

Your activity levels measure distance and climbing. Bhutan adds a third thing to plan for, which is elevation. This trip stays between about 4,300 ft in Punakha and 10,200 ft on Dochu La, a range most active travelers handle comfortably once the first days are paced properly. We would rather map each day to your levels with you than guess at them here.

Everything below is a proposal. We run no set departures, so the routing, the length and the hiking on each day can be shaped to fit your product.

A first walk along the valley floor and up to Kyichu Lhakhang, one of the oldest temples in the country. Kept deliberately easy: the point is to get legs moving after the flights and let everyone settle into the elevation.

Road 90 minTrails above the cityTakin preserve

Ninety minutes of road, then an afternoon hike on the trails above Thimphu. The gentler option is the takin preserve, Bhutan’s national animal, on flat paths.

Pass 10,200 ftWalk among the chortensDown to 4,300 ft

A walk among the chortens at Dochu La, with the eastern Himalaya behind them on a clear morning, then the drive down to the warm Punakha valley. Punakha Dzong in the late afternoon light.

1 night · Punakha

Punakha — river, bridge and villages
River valleySuspension bridgeVillage walking

Along the river, across the long suspension bridge and on through the villages. The longer option climbs to a hillside temple above the valley; the gentler one stays on the valley floor.

1 night · Punakha
8Day
Punakha to our lodge in the Khotokha valley
About an hourOur own propertyAfternoon walk

About an hour on the road to our own lodge in the Khotokha valley, off the standard circuit. An easy afternoon walk across the valley floor to settle in.

1 night · our lodge, Khotokha
9Day
Khotokha — two options from the lodge
Longer hikeOr the farm and the villageHot stone bath

Two options offered at breakfast: a longer hike into the hills around the valley, or the farm, the village and the valley floor. The group splits and rejoins at dinner, and the day ends with a traditional hot stone bath.

1 night · our lodge, Khotokha
10Day
Phobjikha — the glacial valley
Gangtey nature trailFlat and wideCranes late Oct – Mar

Over to the wide glacial valley of Phobjikha and the Gangtey nature trail, an easy walk across open country, and back to the lodge for the evening. From late October into March this is where the black-necked cranes winter; a departure outside those months will not see them, and it is better to say so than to imply it.

1 night · our lodge, Khotokha
