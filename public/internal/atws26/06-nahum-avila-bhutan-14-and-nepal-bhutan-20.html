The short version

We are your Gustavo for the Himalaya

You work with Gustavo at Chile Nativo in Patagonia: an operator who owns the lodge and runs the ground operation, so there is one relationship and one point of accountability rather than a chain of them. That is exactly what we are in this region, and it is why the analogy landed at the table.

Your flagship is Andes, Amazon and Galápagos in a single journey — three places whose difference from each other is the product. So the 20-day below is not a Bhutan trip with Nepal bolted onto the front. It is built the way your Ecuador trip is built, and the contrast between the two countries is the thing being sold.

What we carry: ground operations and DMC in one. Arrivals, transfers, all domestic movement, every government permit, guides, accommodation, and a single contact. You sell and you lead; we operate.

5Day
Tiger's Nest
Real climb, graded trailThree ways to do itNobody excluded

The one image everyone in the group already has in their head. It is a real climb, but on a well-graded trail, and the group splits naturally into three: the full ascent, the halfway teahouse, or the valley below with the vehicle.

For a 45–70 group this is the day that decides whether people feel included or sorted. Framed as three legitimate versions of the same morning rather than a pass and a fail, it becomes the highlight of the trip for everyone. Framed badly, it is the day half the group sits out.

Why the two countries belong in one trip

Kathmandu is a spiritual explosion. Bhutan is quiet.

Neither one lands the same way without the other, and that is the sentence to sell the 20-day with. Your clients will not fully register how still Bhutan is unless they have spent three days in Kathmandu first, and they will not appreciate Kathmandu’s intensity if they arrive from a Bhutanese valley.

The 45-minute Kathmandu–Paro flight along the Himalayan wall is the hinge between them, and it is one of the great flights anywhere. Kathmandu is also one of only five access points into Bhutan, so this routing costs nothing in logistics that a single-country trip would not already cost.

Photography

Our Nepal library is Everest-region trekking and Boudhanath, with nothing in between. The three walking days above are deliberately off those circuits, so we hold no photographs of them at all — the Nepal images on this page are all Kathmandu. That is a gap in our library rather than a gap in the trip, and it is worth knowing before this goes in front of a client who will ask what the walking looks like.

The route is a proposal

The Bhutan days are ours to operate and the routing is solid. The three Nepal walking days are shaped but not yet tied to a named route, deliberately — we would rather name it once we have confirmed which valleys we are staffing next season than invent one now.
